Community Guidelines

Effective date: August 17, 2026

Parent Chat works best when people can ask honest questions and share support without fear of harassment. These guidelines apply to posts, comments, messages, profiles, photos, videos, and activity recommendations.

Be respectful

Do not harass, threaten, bully, intimidate, or target people based on protected characteristics. Do not encourage violence, self-harm, or illegal activity.

Keep children safe

Parent Chat is an adults-only service. Do not upload photos or videos that include children. Sexual or exploitative content is strictly prohibited, especially any sexual or exploitative imagery involving a minor, and results in immediate removal and may be reported to authorities.

Share responsibly

Only upload content you own or have permission to share. Do not post private personal information such as home addresses, phone numbers, or identifying details about someone else. Remember that recommendations and activity listings may change; verify details yourself.

No abuse of the service

Do not spam, impersonate another person, scrape the service, attempt unauthorized access, upload malware, manipulate engagement, or use Parent Chat to solicit or defraud others.

How to report

Use the ••• menu on a post or comment and choose Report. You can block an account from the same menu; blocked accounts cannot interact with you. Reports are sent to moderators and reviewed within 24 hours when possible. Contact support.chaniiapps@gmail.com for urgent safety concerns.

Enforcement

Content that violates these guidelines may be removed immediately. Depending on severity and history, we may warn, limit, suspend, or remove an account. Serious or repeated violations may be reported to law enforcement or relevant safety organizations.
